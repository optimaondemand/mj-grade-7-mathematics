As we get ready to explore finding the area of composite figures, it is important to review finding the areas of all these shapes. Composite figures are figures made up of other shapes. To find the areas, we break them down into the known shapes, like rectangles, triangles, and trapezoids.

All shapes are made up of basic shapes like triangles, rectangles, parallelograms, and trapezoids. Knowing how to find the area of these shapes will allow you to find the area or derive the formula for the area of other shapes. It also helps in finding the areas of complex shapes that look like several shapes put together. Practicing the areas of the basic shapes will make you stronger in finding the areas of more complex shapes.
