As we get ready to learn how to find the probability of an event and express it as a fraction, decimal, or percentage, it is important to review how to convert fractions to decimals and percentages and vice versa.


In mathematics, numbers can be represented in different ways. Fractions and decimals are two common ways to represent the same value. In this lesson, we will explore how to rewrite a fraction as a terminating decimal and how to rewrite a terminating decimal as a fraction.


Rewriting a Fraction as a Terminating Decimal and Percentage

A terminating decimal is a decimal that ends or has a finite number of digits after the decimal point. Not all fractions can be turned into terminating decimals, but many can. Here's how you can do it:

  1. Divide the Numerator by the Denominator: Take the fraction's numerator and divide it by the denominator using long division or a calculator.
  2. Write Down the Result: The result of the division is the decimal representation of the fraction.

A percentage is the ratio of a number out of 100 or multiplying a decimal by 100.

After you rewrite a fraction as a decimal, you can multiply it by 100 and rewrite it as a percentage.

Rewriting a Fraction as a Repeating Decimal

A repeating decimal is a decimal that has a digit or group of digits that repeat indefinitely. Here's how to rewrite a fraction as a repeating decimal:

  1. Divide the Numerator by the Denominator: Use long division or a calculator to divide the numerator by the denominator.
  2. Identify the Repeating Part: Look for a pattern that repeats in the decimal.
  3. Write Down the Decimal: Use a bar (vinculum) over the repeating part.
