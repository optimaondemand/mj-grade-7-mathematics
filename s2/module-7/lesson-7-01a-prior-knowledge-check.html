Understanding Fractions and Percentages

First, let's recall what fractions and percentages are:

  • Fraction: Represents a part of a whole. It's written with two numbers, the numerator (top number) and the denominator (bottom number).
  • Percentage: A way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The symbol "%" is used to denote percentage.


Converting Fractions to Percentages

To convert a fraction to a percentage, follow these simple steps:

  1. Divide the numerator by the denominator to get a decimal.
  2. Multiply the decimal by 100.
  3. Add the percentage symbol (%) to the result.
