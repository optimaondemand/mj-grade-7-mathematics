📖 PRIOR KNOWLEDGE

In a previous lesson, you learned how adding or removing a data value from a set of data affects the measures of center and variation. As we get ready to explore how outliers affect the meaures of center and variation, it will be helpful to review the effects of adding and removing data values.

 

Effects on Measures of Center

  • Mean: Adding or removing a value can change the mean (average). If the new value is higher or lower than the current mean, it will pull the mean up or down.
  • Median: The median may or may not change, depending on where the added or removed value falls in relation to the other values.
  • Mode: The mode changes only if the frequency of the most common value is affected.


Effects on Measures of Variation

  • Range: Adding a value can increase the range if it's outside the current range. Removing a value can decrease the range if it's the highest or lowest value.
  • IQR: The IQR is less likely to change significantly unless the added or removed value is near the first or third quartile.

Conclusion

Adding or removing values from a data set can significantly impact the measures of center and variation. Understanding these changes helps us accurately interpret data and make informed decisions based on our analysis. Remember, the impact depends on how the new or removed value compares to the existing data set.
